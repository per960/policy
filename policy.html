Privacy Policy for K-OTT

Effective Date: 07/07/2026

Kancharla Media Pvt Ltd is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application (K-OTT) handles your information.

Information We Collect

Our app does not require traditional user accounts. To authorize your device for media playback, we collect essential technical data:

  • A randomly generated Device ID.
  • The Activation Code provided to you.

We do not collect, store, or process personal names, emails, payment details, or location data.

How We Use Information

The collected information is used exclusively to verify your device's activation status and provide you with uninterrupted access to the K-OTT media dashboard.

Data Security, Retention, and Deletion

We ensure that our app is designed to respect your privacy. Kancharla Media Pvt Ltd uses secure databases to store your device activation status and protects it against unauthorized access.

Data Deletion Request: Because we do not link your Device ID to any personal identity, we retain this data only as long as your activation code is valid. If you wish to have your Device ID and activation history manually deleted from our servers at any time, please email us at the contact address below with your 10-digit activation code.

Third-Party Services

Our app acts as a media aggregator and utilizes third-party APIs (such as YouTube embed players) to stream video content. We do not host these video files on our servers. Your interaction with embedded media is governed by the privacy policies of those third-party providers:

  • Google / YouTube Privacy Policy

Children's Privacy

Our app is not targeted at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted with an updated effective date.
